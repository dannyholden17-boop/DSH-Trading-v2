What Flux Portfolio is — and isn't.

Is my connection read-only?

Yes — read-only by default. A new brokerage connection lets Flux see your positions and history and nothing more. It cannot place or cancel a trade unless you explicitly enable trading and set your rules.

Can Flux trade for me?

Only if you turn it on. Autopilot executes solely within guardrails you define — max position size, daily loss limit and an allowed-ticker list — after you approve the strategy. It logs everything, and you can pause it anytime, which returns the account to read-only.

Which brokers work?

Major US brokerages, connected through secure aggregation. The list shown on this page is illustrative for the demo, and available brokers can change over time.

Is my data safe?

Connections are encrypted in transit and at rest, and Flux never sees your broker password. We do not sell your portfolio data, and you can revoke access at any time from Flux or your broker.

Is this a sample portfolio?

Yes. Every figure shown on this page — the total value, the holdings, the allocation and the brief — is a simulated sample for illustration. It is not a real account and not a record of actual holdings.

Is this investment advice?

No. Flux is research and automation software, not a broker-dealer and not an adviser. Insights are probabilistic estimates, not recommendations, and every decision — with its outcome — is yours.
