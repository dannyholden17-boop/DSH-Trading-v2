One broker · Robinhood

Connect Robinhood, and keep your hand on every order.

Flux supports one brokerage, and it connects somewhere that may surprise you: not here. The link is made inside Claude, using Claude's own Robinhood connector — the same connector the desk's analysts and traders already run on. This site never sees your Robinhood login, and it never places a trade.

  1. Connect Robinhood to Claude

    In Claude, open Settings → Connectors and authorise Robinhood. The authorisation is between you and Robinhood; Claude holds the session, not Flux.

    In Claude

  2. Read the desk's ruling

    The Executive publishes every green-lit call with its entry, stop, target and the reason it was taken at that size — plus the ones it refused.

    On Flux

  3. Ask Claude to work it

    Hand Claude the call and it can pull your real positions, check the name against your book, and prepare the order through the Robinhood connector.

    In Claude

  4. You place it

    Claude asks before anything is submitted, and Robinhood asks again. Two confirmations, both yours. Nothing is ever placed on your behalf while you are away.

    In Claude

What this gets you

  • The desk's research applied to the positions you actually hold, not a hypothetical book.
  • Real balances, real positions and real order history, read straight from Robinhood.
  • Claude can price and stage an order, then wait for you.
  • Every ruling Flux publishes stays readable and checkable against what you did next.

What it does not do

  • Flux never receives, stores or transmits your Robinhood credentials. There is nothing to type in on this site.
  • Nothing on Flux places an order. The site has no path to your brokerage at all.
  • Autopilot and the desk never touch a real account. They trade a simulated book with virtual money, and that does not change when you connect Robinhood.
  • Flux is not a broker-dealer and gives no investment advice.
Why it works this way.

A website cannot drive a Claude connector — connectors run inside Claude, next to the model, which is exactly where the safety check belongs. Routing through Claude means the thing that reasons about the trade is the same thing that has to ask you before placing it. It also means Flux stays out of the credential business entirely, which is the arrangement we would want as customers.

If you would rather keep everything simulated, you do not need any of this: the paper terminal runs on real market data and virtual money, with no brokerage attached.
