Questions

What people ask about the AI.

Does Flux AI place trades by itself?

No. It researches, forecasts and drafts orders, but nothing routes until you confirm it. There is no setting that lets it trade autonomously — that's a deliberate design decision, not a missing feature.

Is this just ChatGPT with a stock prompt?

No. The language layer is one part of it. Underneath are six purpose-built models — a sequence forecaster, a volatility-regime classifier, a sentiment scorer, a Monte-Carlo portfolio simulator, a signal re-ranker, and a report writer — plus direct access to your live positions and the engines' output.

Can it predict the market?

No, and it won't claim to. It produces probabilistic forecasts with explicit confidence scores, and shows you the historical base rate behind any setup. Confidence is a measure of model agreement, not a guarantee.

What does it actually read?

Price and volume history, the options tape, SEC filings and 8-Ks, earnings material, the news wire, and your own portfolio and order history. It cites which of those a given answer drew on.

Where does my data go?

Your positions and activity stay tied to your account. Flux connects to your broker with your own credentials and never takes custody of funds.
