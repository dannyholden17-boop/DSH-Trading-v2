Questions

The things everyone asks first.

Does Flux place trades on its own?

No — not unless you tell it to. By default it researches and surfaces signals. You choose paper or live, and every live order passes through the Risk engine's guardrails first. You're always in control.

Where does the data come from?

Flux connects to real market data and, for live trading, your own broker via a secure connection. Your keys stay yours — we never take custody of funds.

Do I need to know how to code or backtest?

No. Ask a question in plain English; Flux assembles the research, runs the backtest, and hands you the result with one-tap actions.

Is this financial advice?

No. Flux is research and automation tooling. It surfaces information and runs the workflows you configure. Every decision — and its outcome — is yours.

Can I cancel anytime?

Anytime, in one click. Start on the free Scout plan for as long as you like and upgrade only if it earns it.
