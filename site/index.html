FAQ

The honest answers.

Does Flux trade my money automatically?

No. Autopilot trades its own $10,000 simulated paper account so you can watch it work. It only trades your real brokerage if you explicitly enable it and set the rules — position limits, loss limits, allowed tickers — and you can pause it anytime.

Is the $10,000 fund real money?

No — it's a simulated paper account with virtual money. It exists so the AI's decisions and track record are fully transparent. Simulated results are hypothetical and do not represent real trading or guarantee future outcomes.

Is Flux investment advice?

No. Flux is research and automation software, not investment advice, and is not a broker-dealer. It shows evidence and probabilities; the decisions are always yours.

What is Flux Brain / the Obsidian connection?

Flux Brain lets you connect your Obsidian vault and trade journal so the AI answers in your context — remembering your theses and rules. Your vault stays private and live sync runs through the desktop app.

Which brokerages can I connect?

Major US brokers via secure aggregation — read-only by default. The list shown on the Portfolio page is illustrative; you can revoke access anytime.

Is my data safe?

Connections are encrypted and read-only unless you turn on trading. We never sell your data, and you can disconnect whenever you want.
