Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026
Flux is a research and paper-trading platform. We collect the minimum needed to run your account and never sell your personal data.

Who we are

Flux is operated by DSH Trading ("Flux", "we", "us"). This policy explains what we collect when you use dsh-trading.com and the Flux apps, and how we handle it.

What we collect

  • Account details — your email address and display name when you sign up (including via Google sign-in).
  • App data — your paper-trading activity, watchlists, price alerts, preferences, and the notes you teach Fluxi. This is stored so your desk works across devices.
  • Brokerage data (only if you connect one) — if you link a brokerage, we access your holdings and balances read-only to show your portfolio. We never move your money or place real trades unless you explicitly enable and configure it.
  • Technical data — basic logs and device/browser information used to keep the service secure and working.

How we use it

  • To run your account, save your paper portfolio, and personalize Fluxi's answers.
  • To operate the AI assistant. When you chat with Fluxi, your message and relevant market context are sent to our AI provider to generate a response. We don't use your conversations to train third-party models.
  • To process subscription payments (handled by Stripe — we never store your full card details).
  • To keep the platform secure and to fix problems.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not place real trades or move funds without your explicit, configured permission.
  • We do not share your brokerage data with advertisers.

Third parties we rely on

We use Supabase (database & authentication), Netlify (hosting), Stripe (payments), Anthropic (the AI assistant), and — only if you connect one — a brokerage-aggregation provider. Each processes data solely to provide their part of the service.

Your choices

  • You can edit your name, reset your paper account, or delete your account at any time from Settings.
  • You can disconnect a linked brokerage whenever you want.
  • You can ask us to delete your data by emailing the address below.

Security

Connections are encrypted in transit. Brokerage links are read-only by default. No system is perfectly secure, so we encourage a strong, unique password.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email support@dsh-trading.com.

Flux is research and automation software, not investment advice, and is not a broker-dealer. This policy may be updated; material changes will be posted here.
