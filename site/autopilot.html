Every trade walks the same five gates.

Nothing is discretionary and nothing is hidden. A fill only lands in the log after it clears scan, score, risk and execution — and each step is written down.

01

Scan

Six engines — Scanner, Catalyst, Options, Technicals, Risk and Backtest — sweep the tape for breakouts, flow, catalysts and level reclaims.

02

Score & rank

AlphaRank re-scores every surviving signal against years of outcomes and ranks the candidates by model agreement, not by how loud they look.

03

Risk gate

The Risk engine checks size, stop distance, correlation and total exposure. An oversized or correlated trade is blocked before it exists.

04

Execute (paper)

The survivor is filled inside the $10,000 paper account with simulated slippage — never a real order, never real dollars.

05

Log & report

The fill, the reason and the running P/L are written to the live log and rolled into the hourly and daily reports.

Engine outputs are probabilistic estimates, not predictions or recommendations. Every action described here happens inside a simulated paper account.

The fair questions.

Is this real money?

No. The Flux Fund is a simulated $10,000 paper account using virtual money. It exists as a transparency tool — a live demonstration of how the AI thinks and trades — not as a real portfolio. Every number you see is hypothetical.

Will it trade MY account?

Only if you opt in and set the rules. By default Autopilot trades nothing but its own paper money. If you connect a brokerage, you define the mandate — max size, loss limits, allowed tickers — and you can pause or stop it at any moment.

Can I see every trade?

Yes. The live log shows each fill as it happens, and the hourly and daily reports write up what the fund did and why. The whole point of the paper account is that nothing is hidden.

Is this financial advice?

No. Flux is research and automation software — not a broker-dealer and not an investment adviser. Nothing it produces is personalised advice or a recommendation, and every decision on your real account is yours.

What strategies does it use?

A multi-engine mix: momentum and breakout scanning, options flow, catalyst-driven setups and mean-reversion — each signal cross-checked and then risk-gated for size, stop, correlation and exposure before it's allowed to trade.

Can it lose money?

Yes. The simulated results include losing trades and losing days, because that's honest. Trading involves risk of loss, including loss of principal, and no strategy — human or AI — removes it.
