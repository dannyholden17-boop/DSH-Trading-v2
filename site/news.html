How it works

How Flux reads the news.

A person can skim ten headlines and miss the one that mattered. Flux reads the whole wire, scores every line, and only surfaces what actually touches your book.

01

Ingest

The wire, filings and exchange notices are pulled the moment they publish, deduplicated so one story syndicated forty times counts once.

02

Score sentiment

NewsSense — a FinBERT model — reads each item and scores it bullish, bearish or neutral, with the confidence attached rather than a vibe.

03

Link to your book

Every item is mapped to the tickers it names and cross-checked against your positions and watchlist, so the feed is about your exposure, not the whole market's.

04

Surface what matters

Ranked by impact and relevance, the loud-but-irrelevant is muted and the quiet-but-material rises — then the Reporter writes it up in a line you can act on.

Sentiment scores describe model agreement with a headline's tone. They are not predictions, not a probability of profit, and no summary here is a recommendation to buy or sell.
