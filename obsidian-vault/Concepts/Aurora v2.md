# Aurora v2

A deeper rethink than [[Aurora]]. Keeps the dashboard model but **rebuilds the
information hierarchy** — what's surfaced first, how panels relate, where the
eye lands. A middle ground between the familiar terminal and the radical
[[Conversational Cockpit]].

> [!note] When to pick this
> If Aurora feels too timid but the cockpit feels too far, v2 is the
> evolution: recognizably DSH, meaningfully better organized.

- **Paradigm:** dashboard, re-architected
- **Risk:** medium
