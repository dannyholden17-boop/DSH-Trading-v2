# Aurora

The most conservative of the three redesigns: a **polished restyle** of the
classic DSH terminal. Same mental model — dashboard, sidebar, panels — but with a
modern dark "aurora" theme, cleaner hierarchy, and better spacing.

> [!note] When to pick this
> If the goal is to *modernize without retraining users*, Aurora is the safe
> landing spot. Everything is where a returning user expects it.

- **Paradigm:** familiar dashboard, restyled
- **Risk:** low
- **Mockup:** `design-concepts/01-aurora.html`
